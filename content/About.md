With over 4 years of experience in research and teaching followed by 5 years in technology industry, Ankit works as Software Quality Engineer at a Canadian Health Tech firm, building testing infrastructure and validating software for health care research . In his free time, he likes to write, tinker with new technology and build stuff at [BhandLab] (https://medium.com/bhandlab). In addition, he provides tech consultancy to businesses for relevant technical problems. 

With background in both academic teaching and employee training, he is passionate about creating novel learning and gaming experiences using technology.

He comes from Lucknow, India, where he spent most of his childhood. After completing his undergraduate degree in Engineering from [Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee](https://www.iitr.ac.in/), he moved to Canada in 2016 for his PhD program at [Western University](https://www.uwo.ca/). 

Following his graduation, he switched to tech industry to solve real-world problems using technology.

When not working, Ankit enjoys tutoring grade-school students, writes poetry, philosophical prose and short stories, and loves to sketch manga drawings.
