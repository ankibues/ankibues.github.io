With over four years of experience in research and teaching, followed by five years in the technology industry, Ankit works as a Software Quality Engineer at a Canadian health technology firm. He builds testing infrastructure and validates software used in healthcare research. In his free time, he enjoys writing, experimenting with new technologies, and building projects at [BhandLab](https://medium.com/bhandlab). He also provides technology consulting to businesses.

With a background in both academic teaching and employee training, he is passionate about creating novel learning and gaming experiences using technology.

He grew up in Lucknow, India. After completing his undergraduate engineering degree at the [Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee](https://www.iitr.ac.in/), he moved to Canada in 2016 to pursue a PhD at [Western University](https://www.uwo.ca/).

Following graduation, he transitioned into the technology industry to solve real-world problems.

When not working, Ankit enjoys tutoring grade-school students, writing poetry, philosophical prose, and short stories, and sketching manga.
